How It Works

A Simple, Predictable Process

Step 1

Share Your Files

Send sketches, PDFs, scans or site photos along with your scope and target turnaround.

Step 2

Scope & Quote

You get a clear quote and timeline before any work begins — no surprise revisions to pricing.

Step 3

Drafting & Review

Drawings are produced and shared for your comments; revisions are addressed directly with you.

Step 4

Final Delivery

Final files delivered in the formats you need — DWG, PDF, or vector — ready for your next step.

A note on scope: We draft and prepare documentation to your specification. Where a jurisdiction requires a stamped or sealed drawing, that review and seal is provided by your project's licensed architect or engineer of record — not by AKP Design Studio.

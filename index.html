    About AKP Design Studio

    A Studio Built Around Two Disciplines

    AKP Design Studio is a specialized drafting and visualization studio operating under AKP Engineers & Construction. We combine technical 2D CAD and construction documentation with professional 3D architectural visualization to help clients move from drawings to presentation-ready visuals.

    Established July 2023 in Dhaka, Bangladesh, we focus on serving contractors, architects, engineers, builders and property professionals across the United States.

    The studio is led by two specialists — one in technical CAD documentation, one in architectural visualization — working together under one coordinated process.

    How It Works

    How It Works

    The same simple, predictable process whether you're ordering 2D drafting, 3D visualization, or both.

    Step 1

    Share Your Files

    Send sketches, PDFs, scans or a project brief along with your scope and target turnaround.

    Step 2

    Scope & Quote

    You get a clear quote and timeline before any work begins — no surprise revisions to pricing.

    Step 3

    Drafting / Modeling & Review

    Work is produced and shared for your comments; revisions are addressed directly with the specialist on your project.

    Step 4

    Final Delivery

    Final files delivered in the formats you need — DWG, PDF, vector or rendered images — ready for your next step.

    Professional seals: We draft and prepare documentation to your specification. Where a jurisdiction requires a stamped or sealed drawing, that review and seal is provided by your project's licensed architect or engineer of record — not by AKP Design Studio.
